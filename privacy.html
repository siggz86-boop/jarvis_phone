Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Jarvis is a private personal assistant built and used by one person, Brad, for his own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's data is collected.

What Jarvis can access

Only when Brad signs in and agrees, Jarvis can use his Google account to:

  • read, search, label and archive his email, and write drafts (it never sends email and never deletes it);
  • read his calendar and add or change events he asks for;
  • find, create and save files and folders in his Google Drive (it never deletes his files);
  • read his contacts, and read or write his tasks, spreadsheets and documents when he asks.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to do what Brad asks, in the moment. Requests pass through Brad's own private server and Anthropic's Claude service to produce answers. The data is not sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

What is stored

Jarvis's sign-in to Google is kept in a private, encrypted credential vault. Jarvis may keep short notes Brad asks it to remember. Email, calendar and file contents are not copied into any separate database.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops Jarvis reaching the account immediately.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
